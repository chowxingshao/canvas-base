# Project brief

## Client and purpose

Acme Studio needs a concise portfolio that helps prospective clients start a project conversation.

## Site type

Portfolio.

## Audience

Design-conscious founders comparing studios; they should review selected work and contact Acme.

## Pages and sections

Home, selected projects, studio, and contact.

## Content sources

Approved case-study notes and image assets supplied by the client.

## Tone and voice

Direct, thoughtful, calm. Avoid jargon and exaggerated claims.

## Visual references (optional)

Editorial portfolios with generous reading space.

## Must-haves and must-nots

Use the approved wordmark; avoid autoplay video.

## Motion

Subtle entrances only.

## Technical

Public form provider endpoint, no analytics, English and Malay, launch date to be confirmed.
